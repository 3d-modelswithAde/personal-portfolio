Connecting Enquiries Across n8n, HubSpot, Gmail and Telegram

A customer-enquiry workflow that connects a CRM, email and a Telegram group through n8n.

The problem

Enquiries arrive in one place, get recorded in another and are answered somewhere else. Moving details between tools by hand is slow and easy to get wrong, so I built a workflow to connect them.

The workflow

n8n is the orchestration layer. It connects to HubSpot, which holds the contact records, to Gmail, for email, and to a Telegram bot that I added to a group, so the workflow can notify people there.

What it does now, and what could be extended

Today this is a customer-enquiry workflow. It is not a community onboarding system. The tools and the pattern carry over to that use, as the next section describes.

From Customer Enquiries to Community Onboarding

These are potential applications of the same principles. None of them is built yet.

  • Capture new-member information when someone joins.
  • Record member details in a CRM such as HubSpot or Airtable.
  • Send welcome emails and relevant resources.
  • Notify moderators in Telegram about new members or unresolved requests.
  • Direct members to resources, events or community spaces.
