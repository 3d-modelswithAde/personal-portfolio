How I thought it through

Reactivate

Took over an account that had been dormant for a year and decided what to build first, with no brief.

Audit

Compared local food and retail brands to find underserved niches, and ran on-page SEO checks on profile visibility.

Position

Moved the brand from “a local restaurant” to the go-to spot for 9-to-5ers and remote workers who want affordable, delicious meals they can order in or dine in comfortably. I chose this because banks and ministries surround the kitchen, and those same offices were already booking us for meetings and quarterly meetups.

Produce

Built a monthly content calendar around three pillars (educational, promotional, relatable). Each entry had a hook, caption, CTA, sound and hashtags, and became a reel, carousel or picture.

Distribute and convert

Scheduled posts across Instagram and TikTok, tracked link clicks, and moved inquiries to paid orders.

Measure and iterate

Logged hooks, sounds, hashtags and searched keywords for every reel against its views, then wrote recommendations for the next cycle. What the data showed: reels drive most interactions, educational carousels perform well, and meme-plus-caption posts work.

Launch under pressure

Ran the annual Christmas Menu campaign on a tight deadline, which brought in half of the season’s revenue.
