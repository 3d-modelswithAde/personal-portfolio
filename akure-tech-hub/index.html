Moments from the community

Turning an event into a conversation

After an online space event, I asked attendees what they took away. One said it changed his view of simulation, because it is not a perfect system that carries straight into real life. Another said AI should be used to work through a project and be verified, not only to collect information.

Promoting hands-on workshops

I announced a SolidWorks workshop by asking how cars and aircraft get modelled, then answering it: everything starts with computer-aided design. The post named the engineer facilitating and the venue near FUTA’s south gate.

Answering practical questions

Members asked where to find a component urgently and where to get 3D printing done. The community pointed them to nearby electronics stores, an online hardware shop and a mechatronics lab.

How I thought it through

Run the space

Managed a 114-member group for Mechatrics community members, keeping it useful for learning and not only announcements.

Moderate the technical talk

Kept discussions on robotics, machine learning and 3D mechanical modelling active, which brought 30+ contributions to each session.

Close the loop after events

Followed up with attendees to ask for their takeaways, so one-way events became conversations.

Bring people offline

Used the group to promote physical workshops, and turnout at them rose by 20%.

Answer fast

Treated members’ practical questions as part of the job and pointed them to local resources quickly.
