MT Babs Seminar Coordination

Lead Organizer & Cross-Functional Committee Director · 2026. I coordinated the bimonthly virtual seminars for MT Babs Foundation scholars with a team of volunteer undergraduates who had different departments, universities and schedules.

Results at a glance

17volunteers coordinated as one committee
5divisions: publicity, speakers, technical, registration and seminar coordination
30scholars at a virtual seminar across multiple universities
100%attendee satisfaction score
0downtime during the seminar

The coordination challenge

Different departments

Volunteers came from fields as far apart as food science, metallurgical and materials engineering, and medicine.

Different universities

The team spanned several universities, so no one shared a campus or a timetable.

Different schedules

Everyone was a student with their own exams and deadlines, so the work had to fit around them.

How I thought it through

Define the structure

Wrote a volunteer structure with clear duties. The seminar coordinator handled overall supervision, timelines, team coordination and progress reports. Speaker engagement, publicity and technical teams each had their own list.

Match people to strengths

Asked every volunteer to introduce themselves with name, level, department, university and skill, so I could place each person where they would be most useful.

Fill the gaps

Noticed which teams were full and which were short of people, and asked for volunteers where they were needed.

Give teams one place to report

Set up a single group where every team posted reports and worked together, and asked team leads to say upfront if they could not take on the work.

Rehearse and prepare for problems

Enforced strict dry-run schedules and contingency plans, which led to a seminar with zero downtime.

Document for the next team

Compiled post-event reports, survey analysis and a reusable playbook for the Foundation’s executives.
